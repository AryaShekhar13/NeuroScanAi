# Dala Constellation — Design Direction

## Ground-Truth Reference

The supplied `DESIGN.md` is the definitive creative specification for this project. The website must express **“a constellation floating on black velvet”**: an asymmetrical, typographic dark-stage environment in which pure black is an active material and a field of multicolored triangular particles visualizes distributed workplace knowledge.

## Chosen Design Philosophy: The Distributed Intelligence Stage

### Design Movement

Editorial Swiss minimalism translated into an interactive dark-stage software brand. Hierarchy comes from scale, rhythm, and negative space rather than panels, shadows, or visual clutter.

### Core Principles

1. **The void is structural.** Every major section remains pure black, allowing typography and chromatic particles to appear suspended in space.
2. **Scale creates hierarchy.** Headlines use regular weight at oversized scales and compact tracking; body copy is intentionally light and breathable.
3. **The constellation is the product metaphor.** Outlined triangular particles form an organic neural-cloud shape and drift subtly through the surrounding canvas.
4. **One decisive action per view.** Electric Iris is exclusive to the primary request-access action and a handful of active details.

### Color Philosophy

Black provides silence and dimensionality. Bone White carries all primary language. Electric Iris signals agency and brand identity, while Saffron Spark introduces selective moments of human warmth. Teal, magenta, and blue exist only inside the procedural constellation where they imply multiple sources converging into intelligence.

### Layout Paradigm

An asymmetric sequence of two-column editorial compositions: dense thought on one side, an uninterrupted void or particle field on the other. The scroll alternates emphasis rather than flattening content into repeated grids.

### Signature Elements

- A living cloud of fine outlined triangles, concentrated into a neural/brain-like silhouette.
- Small saffron section labels paired with large, sculptural white statements.
- A sharp angular Dala mark that echoes one constellation particle.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions are quiet acknowledgements rather than decoration. Primary actions compress slightly on press; navigation and text links brighten without acquiring containers. The constellation responds gently to pointer position while preserving calm.

### Animation

Ambient particles drift at long, low-contrast intervals. The constellation breathes through delicate opacity and positional shifts. Content enters with short vertical reveals under 300ms; all non-essential animation respects reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

The supplied PPNeueMontreal style is represented with `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, and sans-serif fallbacks. Display typography uses regular weight, tight negative tracking, and responsive 78–113px-scale behavior. Body text is 18px at an extra-light 200 weight where available. Small navigation and labels are uppercase, semibold, and tracked.

### Brand Essence

**Dala is a workplace intelligence layer for teams that want to ask once and draw on collective knowledge.**

Personality: **incisive, spacious, quietly brilliant.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines are direct and affirmative. CTAs are short and confident. Microcopy avoids generic SaaS phrasing and explains value through the team’s working reality.

Example lines:

> Your workplace has the answer. Just ask Dala for it.

> Turn scattered expertise into a conversation your team can actually use.

### Wordmark & Logo

The logo combines a compact, violet angular triangle fragment with a small white Dala wordmark. The mark is always recognizable without the wordmark and scales into the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color

**Electric Iris — `#8052ff`**

## Style Decisions

- No surface cards, borders, dividers, gradients on UI elements, or drop shadows.
- Keep every persistent text surface on black for reliable contrast.
- Use custom procedural graphics rather than stock imagery in the hero and information sections.
- On mobile, retain editorial asymmetry through offset visual placement rather than collapsing content into centered blocks.
